PROJECT 03 / DIGITAL AWARENESS

Protect what matters.

A beginner-friendly reminder that small digital habits can make a meaningful difference.

✓

PAUSE · CHECK · PROTECT

Your digital life
deserves care.

01

Use strong, unique passwords

Choose long passwords or passphrases and avoid reusing the same password across important accounts. Where available, use a password manager.

02

Turn on multi-factor authentication

Use an additional verification step for important accounts whenever the service supports it. Never share one-time codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

03

Think before you click

Check the sender and destination of unexpected links. Be cautious with urgent requests, unfamiliar attachments, and messages asking for sensitive information.

04

Protect your creative work

Keep backup copies of important media, limit access to private files, and agree on how files will be shared before sending them to others.

This educational demo provides general awareness tips. It is not a professional security audit or a substitute for specialist advice.
